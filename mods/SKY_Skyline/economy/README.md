# SKY economy (Central Economy)

Generated by `gen_economy.py` from `assets/skyspec.py`. Formats were checked against the
vanilla `dayzOffline.chernarusplus` files from BohemiaInteractive/DayZ-Central-Economy
(DayZ 1.29). Every category, usage, value and tag exists in `cfglimitsdefinition.xml`.

## Install into a mission
1. Copy `sky_ce/` to `<mission>\sky_ce\` and add `cfgeconomycore_snippet.xml` into `<economycore>`.
   `<ce folder>` loads `types`, `spawnabletypes` and `events` files only.
2. Merge `mapgroupproto_sky.xml` groups into `<mission>\mapgroupproto.xml`. Proto files can't go in a CE folder.
3. Merge `placement/out/cfgeventspawns_snippet.xml` (or `out_district/`) into `<mission>\cfgeventspawns.xml`. Positions depend on the site.
   For a district also merge `zombie_territories_snippet.xml` into `<mission>\env\zombie_territories.xml` (one `<zone>` inside the `<territory>` that holds the `InfectedCity` zones).
4. Add the exported `Land_SKY_*` lines to `mapgrouppos.xml` (see `placement/README.md`, step 3).

Batch 5: prop groups use the vanilla `lootshelves` pattern (tag `shelves`, small per-point range/height:
`skyspec.LOOT` points `(x, y, z, range, height)`). Props are spawned per floor by the layout generator
(`FURNISH` sets), so their loot positions come from the same `ExportProxyData` export as the towers
(placement/README.md section 3) - **unverified for spawned props, PENDING B7**. Usages/categories/tags
are checked against vanilla `cfglimitsdefinition.xml`; `test_kit.py` checks every point is on open
floor (not in Geometry, the core or a furnish prop) and every prop point rests on a collision surface of
its prop (locker floor and shelf, desk top, counter, worktop).
Sofa, ServerRack, VendingMachine, ExtinguisherCabinet, Cubicle and Bed carry no loot (decorative / solid, or
the floor already has points there): a furnished office floor stays at about 9 loot items, not 16 (perf batch-5 M3).
`sky_layout.py` adds the loot items (sum of lootmax of every spawned class) to the entity count it checks
against `ENTITY_CAP`.

Each stacked floor is its own object, so it gets its own `mapgrouppos` entry and loot roll ("per floor").
All five office floors share one group. Different loot per floor (for example executive top floors)
needs a distinct floor class, which is planned for the kit scale-up.
Points come from `skyspec.LOOT`. `assets/blender/test_towera.py` checks that each point has a floor
under it and nothing solid around it.

## Roof event (`sky_ce/events.xml`: `StaticSKYRoofDrop`)
This copies the vanilla `StaticAirplaneCrate` pattern: fixed positions, with `StaticObj_Misc_SupplyBox1/2_DE`
children carrying 3-6 loot each. The positions are the helipad roof's `roof_drop_N` points, at roof height (`y`).
A helicopter crash (`Wreck_UH1Y`) is **not** used on Tower A: the 24 m roof with its core penthouse is too
small for the wreck. Keep it for the HQ landmark roof.

## Infected density notes (per floor)
* Infected spawn from `zombie_territories.xml` zones / `Infected*` events, on the **terrain**, around players.
  They do not spawn on upper floors. Tower floors fill only when infected path in following players.
  Pathing uses the navmesh, which the spawner updates.
* Recommended: one `InfectedCity` zone per site / district at street level, as generated by
  `placement/sky_layout.py` into `zombie_territories_snippet.xml` (r >= 50 m like the smallest vanilla zone,
  dmin/dmax from the floor mix - see below). About 1-2 infected per floor reach the upper levels during a visit.
* Lobby: highest density (street spawns walk in through the 3 m entrance).
* Floors 1-5: reached via the stairs only. The elevator teleports players, not AI. So expect 0-2 per floor,
  which is a quiet zone for looting.
* Roof: AI can reach it via the stair core. Roof-drop events attract players, not infected.
* The QA gate checks pathing in DayZDiag (navmesh view): stairs, landings, door openings, and no paths through glass.

### Per floor type (batch 5; all numbers are hypotheses for the FPS / gameplay test, PENDING B6)
Mechanism stays the same: vanilla `Infected*` events spawn around players from territory zones
(`cfgenvironment.xml` -> `env/zombie_territories.xml`; zone format verified in the
dayzOffline.chernarusplus reference: `<zone name="InfectedCity" smin smax dmin dmax x z r/>` inside a
`<territory>`). `placement/sky_layout.py` writes **one** such zone per district to
`zombie_territories_snippet.xml` (centre = site centre, `dmax` 10 + 1 per apartment/hotel tower, max 15,
`dmin` = dmax / 2, `r` = towers + 20 m, max 100 m). Choose the zone event by the tower's floor mix and
size it from the table; never place zones on upper floors by hand-editing heights.

| Floor / roof type | Target infected present per floor during a visit | Zone event near the tower | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lobby (all towers) | 3-5 | `InfectedCity` | street spawns walk in through the entrances |
| Office (`office_open`) | 1-2 | `InfectedCity` | open plan, long sightlines: few is enough |
| Apartments | 2-3 | `InfectedCity` / `InfectedVillage` style mix | 4 units, short sightlines, residential loot |
| Hotel | 2-3 | `InfectedCity` | long corridor, 10 doors: ambush pressure without blocking it |
| Mechanical | 0-1 | `InfectedIndustrial` | plant floor, industrial loot, quiet |
| Garden / mechanical roof | 0-1 | - | only followers from the stairs; roof drops attract players |

* The table is a **ceiling per floor, never summed** (perf batch-5 M4). Use **one** `InfectedCity` zone
  per district, centred on the central intersection, `dmax` 10-15 in total; towers 48 m apart would
  otherwise get overlapping zones that stack respawns where players already are. A hotel- or
  apartment-heavy district sits at the top of the range, an office/mechanical one at the bottom.
  Server cost is per infected alive (multi-storey stair paths are the expensive case), so raise it
  only after the B6 measurement.
* Verify (B6): whether territory spawns ever land on upper slabs (navmesh above ground) or only at
  street level; if only street level, upper-floor targets are reached solely by followers and the
  table above is a ceiling, not a target.


## City life (D61)
- `sky_ce/types.xml`: `SKY_Bottle_Beer` 20/10 (Town, Village) and `SKY_Bottle_Vodka` 12/6 (Town, Village, Farm),
  category food like vanilla soda cans, spawned 40-100 % full.
- `mapgroupproto_sky.xml`: `Land_SKY_Landfill` (Industrial / Farm / Village, 8 of 12 points at the foot of the
  mounds, every category) and `Land_SKY_Bridge_Long` (Military, 4 of 8: deck and the two sniper nests).
- Search loot (bins, dumpsters, garbage truck, landfill mounds, mall / cinema costumes, bar stock) is **not** CE:
  the server spawns it from fixed lists in `sky_scripts/.../SKY_Search.c` (30 min per spot, P17). Costumes reuse
  the vanilla suit / dress / mask classes, so no vanilla types entry is overridden.
